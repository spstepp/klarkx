How we pick games at Klarkx

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

We recommend board games, tabletop games, and hobby toys. We do it from published facts, not from a playtest lab we do not have.

What we use

  • The publisher’s product page is the first source for player count, age, play time, components, box size, and awards.
  • If the publisher leaves a fact off the page, we will use an official distributor page or an Amazon listing we have opened, and we will say which one. We do not fill gaps from memory or from BoardGameGeek unless we can open that page and cite it.
  • Amazon links are for buying. A star rating on Amazon is not a reason we picked the game, and we do not quote customer reviews as if they were ours.

What we will not say

We will not say we played a game, tested a game, or asked a family to try one, because we have not. We will not invent a teach time, a “best with” player count, or a box size. If the publisher does not print a box size, we leave it out. If a number cannot be checked, the game stays off the page.

Prices

We do not print dollar prices. Amazon prices move, and we do not yet pull live prices through Amazon’s product API. Next to a product link you will see “check current price.” A budget band (for example, games under $20) is a search we are writing toward, not a promise that a listed game is in that band today.

Amazon links

Klarkx participates in the Amazon Associates program. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Until our Associates tag is live, product links go to plain Amazon product pages and are marked for a tag to be added later. Links use rel="nofollow sponsored".

We also keep a shared exclusion list of products tied to our own Amazon selling. We do not publish an Associates link for anything on that list.

When facts were checked

Each article has a “facts checked” date. Editions change. If you are using a page to buy a gift, look at the publisher page and the Amazon listing again before you order.
