How we pick

We look for games, puzzles and kits that actually get played: quick to learn, fun with family, and small enough to tuck into a stocking.

We haven't played every one of these ourselves. Instead, we stick to what the publisher tells us, like how many people can play, the right ages, how long a game takes and how big the box is. If we can't confirm a detail, we leave it out rather than guess.
