that actually come out of the stocking

A stocking game has one job: get opened and finished before the next present is unwrapped.

If you're stuffing a stocking for a family with kids 8 and up, start with Sushi Go! It's a 15-minute card game. For teens and adults, I'd go with Love Letter: 2 to 6 players, about 20 minutes, and it still works when only two of you are left. If the whole house wants in, Flip 7 is for three or more.

Best for families with kids 8 and up

Sushi Go!

Pass a hand of sushi cards, keep one, and pass the rest. It plays in about 15 minutes with 2 to 5 people, ages 8 and up. The box is small, so this is the one I'd actually expect to fit.

Qwixx

Qwixx is a dice game, and the box is even smaller: 5.25 by 3.75 by 1.2 inches. 2 to 5 players, ages 8 and up, about 15 minutes. You get a chance to gain on every roll, so nobody sits out waiting for a turn.

Qwixx on Amazon (check current price)

The Mind

Nobody talks. You just try to play numbered cards in ascending order and stay in sync. It's cooperative, for 2 to 4 players, ages 8 and up, and it takes about 15 minutes.

The Mind on Amazon (check current price)

Hanabi

You can see everyone else's cards, but not your own. You give short clues and try to build fireworks in order. 2 to 5 players, ages 8 and up, about 30 minutes, so give it a little more time than the others. It won Spiel des Jahres in 2013.

Best for teens

Love Letter

A small deck, a little risk, and you're done in about 20 minutes. 2 to 6 players, ages 10 and up. It's a good one for later, too, when only two of you are still awake.

The Crew: Mission Deep Sea

You win as a team, and you're not supposed to talk freely about the cards in your hand. 3 to 5 players, ages 10 and up, about 20 minutes. The box is 5.1 by 7.1 by 1.7 inches, which is longer than the little tins, so measure the stocking. If it's just the two of you, Kosmos has a 2-player variant.

Coup

Bluff, claim a role, and hope nobody calls you on it. 2 to 6 players, about 15 minutes, ages 13 and up. Save this one for older teens and adults.

Best for adults and party crowds

Flip 7

Flip cards and bank your points before you see the same number twice. It's for 3 or more players, ages 8 and up, about 20 minutes. If the holiday table is crowded, this is your game. It was nominated for Spiel des Jahres in 2025. It doesn't play with two.

Skull

Each person has three flower discs and one skull. You place, bid, and flip, and a skull ends the bid. 3 to 6 players, ages 10 and up, about 30 minutes. It's compact, so it's easy to take along.

Skull on Amazon (check current price)

Love Letter and Coup work at an adult table too. Flip 7 if the group's big. Skull if they like a bluff. Love Letter if you might end up with just two people.

Best for couples

Codenames: Duet

You and a partner give one-word clues and try to find your agents without hitting an assassin. It's the cooperative, two-player version of Codenames, ages 11 and up, about 15 minutes. You can add more people if you want. I don't have a box size, so I wouldn't count on it fitting a stocking.

Jaipur

A trading game for exactly two people. You collect goods, sell them, and try to win two rounds. Ages 10 and up, about 30 minutes. I don't have a box size for this one either, so maybe it goes under the tree.

What makes a good stocking stuffer game

A good stocking game fits the sock, doesn't need a lecture, and wraps up in about half an hour. Sushi Go! and Qwixx come in small boxes. The Crew is travel-size, but that box is about 7 inches long, so check the fit. Most of these are 15 or 20 minutes. Hanabi, Skull, and Jaipur run about 30.

Quick questions

What are good stocking stuffer games for adults?
Flip 7 if you've got three or more people. Coup if they like a bluff and are 13 or older. Skull for a table of 3 to 6. Love Letter if the group might shrink to two. Check the current price on Amazon.
What are good stocking stuffer games for kids or teens?
For kids 8 and up, start with Sushi Go! or Qwixx. Both come in small boxes. The Mind is a quieter cooperative game. For teens, try Love Letter (ages 10+) or The Crew: Mission Deep Sea (ages 10+). Wait until 13 for Coup.
What small card games are good for travel?
The Crew: Mission Deep Sea is called travel-size, at 5.1 by 7.1 by 1.7 inches. Skull is compact and easy to take anywhere. Sushi Go! and Qwixx have small boxes too.
Which small card games work with just 2 players?
Jaipur is two-player only. Codenames: Duet is built for two, and you can add more people. Love Letter, Sushi Go!, Qwixx, The Mind, and Hanabi all start at 2. Flip 7 and Skull need at least 3. The Crew is 3 to 5, with a 2-player variant from Kosmos.
Is a card game a good white elephant gift?
A small game is easy to wrap, and you can play it after the swap. Whether it's a good white elephant gift depends on the room and the price cap. Check the current price so you stay inside the limit, and skip anything with an age line higher than the people at the party.
