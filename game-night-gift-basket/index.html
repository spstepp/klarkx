How to build a
game night gift basket

Start with one main game for the people who will play it, add one or two quicker ones for the nights the big game feels like too much, then add snacks and something cozy.

For a family with kids 8 and up, I'd start with Ticket to Ride. It plays with 2 to 5 people and usually takes 30 to 60 minutes. Sushi Go! is a 15-minute card game for that same group. If you've got at least three people, Flip 7 is another quick one.

The basket formula

I like to keep it to this:

  1. One main game, chosen for the people who will play it
  2. One or two quicker games
  3. A couple of snacks
  4. Something cozy, like a throw they can keep

A regular deck of cards or a score pad is optional. One main game and one or two quicker ones is enough for a night. More than that and you're spending more, and they have more boxes to put away.
